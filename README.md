## 🎮 About the Game

Released on April 3, 2025, Maestro's Cold War 2 is a turn-based grand-strategy and political simulation game developed by Maestro Cinetik. Players lead the United States or the Soviet Union through a replayable Cold War sandbox focused on prestige, budgets, national policies, diplomacy, proxy conflicts, intelligence, and brinkmanship.

## 📖 Detailed Feature Guide

### Movement & Exploration

Maestro's Cold War 2 is a map-based, turn-based strategy game rather than a character-control game. Navigate the world map, select countries or regions, inspect available actions, and advance turns after planning your strategy.

### Tools & Inventory

The game does not use a conventional character inventory. Its main campaign resources include prestige, budget, national policies, technologies, diplomatic relationships, intelligence capabilities, and military or proxy-war activity. Activate resource-related trainer options only after the corresponding values are visible in the interface.

### World & Environment

The world map is the center of the campaign. Use the trainer to experiment with diplomatic influence, treaties, national policies, regional intelligence, technologies, and geopolitical actions. Changes may be recalculated when a turn advances, an event triggers, or a new country interaction is opened.

### Combat & Weapons

Combat is represented through military pressure, proxy wars, interventions, guerrilla activity, and other strategic actions rather than continuous tactical battles. Enable combat-related options before confirming an action, and disable them afterward if you want campaign outcomes to remain closer to the normal rules.
